# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

The developer first: one person managing their own tasks, habits and goals on
an iPhone 17, throughout the day, in short glances between other things. A
public App Store / Play Store release is planned later, so the design must
hold up for strangers who never read the code or the README.

## Product Purpose

Nudge is a local-first task manager for Android and iOS whose promise is that
**important tasks remain visible and keep reminding you until they are
handled.** Success is that nothing important silently rots: put-off work
resurfaces, and finishing it is quick.

## Positioning

Persistence and escalation are the core idea; every other feature supports
them. Tasks that keep being snoozed, deferred or left overdue climb back into
the Focus spotlight, carry a "Put off N times" marker, and regain a daily
"still waiting" nudge after their one-shot reminders have fired. Deferring is
an explicit "not today" swipe rather than letting a task sit overdue.

## Operating Context

- Used on a phone, usually one-handed, in brief sessions: check what's next,
  complete or defer it, capture a new task.
- Capture happens in quick add with natural-language deadlines ("call mom
  tomorrow 5pm") and `#tags`; the full editor is one tap away.
- Much of the interaction happens outside the app: notifications with
  Complete/Snooze actions, persistent alarms, and home-screen widgets ("Up
  next", "Tasks") with deep links.
- A Pomodoro-style focus timer runs alongside work and credits time to tasks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four top-level sections: Focus, Calendar, Habits, Goals; plus Search,
  Completed and Settings.
- Tasks: deadlines as calendar dates plus optional time, priorities,
  subtasks, tags, rich-text details, recurrence (one active occurrence at a
  time, future ones projected), reminders, opt-in persistent alarms, quiet
  hours, escalation.
- Habits (check-in or timer, daily or monthly, checkpoints, streaks) and
  goals (target date, checkpoints or manual progress) are separate subsystems.
- Local-first: no backend, no accounts, no analytics. Calendar integration is
  one-way export only. JSON backup covers tasks only.
- One Flutter codebase for Android and iOS only; web and desktop are
  deliberately unsupported. Light and dark themes exist and the default follows the system
  setting. One visual identity spans both OSes; navigation, controls and
  materials adapt per platform.
- Notification permission is requested only when the first reminder is
  enabled, always after an in-app explanation. The app never claims a
  notification was scheduled when it failed.
- iOS is signed with a free Personal Team: no time-sensitive notification
  entitlement, so alarms cannot break through Focus modes.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name **Nudge** (package name `todo_list` is internal only).
- App icon in `assets/icon/` (`icon.png`, `icon_foreground.png`,
  `icon_monochrome.png`).
- Visual identity, chosen by the user on 2026-10-29 from a Duolingo /
  Headspace-style reference: "the meadow" — bright, colourful and friendly,
  never grey or washed out. Fresh greens, a grass band across the top of each
  tab, chunky rounded cards and buttons with a solid lip, 3D illustrations,
  the rounded Nunito face. `DESIGN.md` records the system. An earlier quiet,
  monochrome "ticket rail" direction was rejected as generic; don't drift
  back toward restraint.
- An original mascot, a chunky sprout (generated by `tool/mascot/mascot.py`
  as Lottie animations), is the app's face; the app icon is the mascot on a
  meadow hill. The user can pick one of eight colour palettes; green is the
  default and the brand. Fluent Emoji 3D illustrations decorate stat tiles
  and empty states.

## Product Principles

1. Nothing important disappears quietly: surface it until it is handled.
2. Pressure without punishment: escalation nudges, it never shames or piles
   up hundreds of overdue rows.
3. Fast in, fast out: capturing and completing a task takes seconds.
4. Honest about the system: never claim a reminder or alarm works when the OS
   prevented it.
5. Local and private: the user's data stays on the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Existing requirements in the codebase: 44pt minimum touch targets, text
tokens at WCAG 4.5:1 in both themes (enforced by tests), screen-reader labels
on task rows, and an app-wide Reduce Motion setting. Dynamic Type / font-scale
support has not been verified.
